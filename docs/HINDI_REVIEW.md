# Hindi text to review (Phase 10)

None of this has been checked by a Hindi speaker. These are the 194 texts that are new or changed in `apps/web/src/i18n/hi.json` since before Phase 10. The earlier text (logins, dashboards) still needs review too.

Words in `{braces}` are filled in by the app. To fix a text, change it in `hi.json`.

The material words in the seed file (`aliasesHi`) are separate and also unchecked.
